What it actually does

Nothing on this page is a promise of a future update — this is the running bot.

🎵

Music and video

Type a song name or paste a link. The bot sends the real audio or video file into the chat, not a link you have to open. .play, .video, .toaudio.

🎭

Stickers and images

Photo or short video becomes a sticker; stickers go back to images; resize, compress, voice notes and GIFs too. .sticker, .toimg, .togif, .vn.

🛡️

Group protection

Anti-link for invite spam, anti-spam rate limiting, anti-delete that reposts a message someone removed, warnings that mute or kick repeat offenders. .antilink, .warn.

🧑‍💼

Admin tools

Promote, demote, add, remove, mute the group, set rules, invite link, tag everyone or only the admins, delete a message, disappearing messages. .tagall, .mute, .del.

🤖

AI answers

Ask a question in English or Sinhala and the bot answers with AI, keeping the last few messages as context. .ai <question>.

💬

Auto replies

Teach the bot your own answers: a keyword and a reply, or your own whole command. Welcome and goodbye messages for groups are included. .autoreply, .addcmd.

⏰

Schedules and notes

Send a message at a set time, keep private notes, set reminders the bot delivers later. .schedule, .remind, .note.

🛠️

Everyday tools

Weather, translate, QR codes, link shortener, Wikipedia, dictionary, polls, reactions, profile pictures, URL checks. .poll, .react, .pp, .qr.

🖼️

Your brand

Your bot gets its own name, its own profile picture, its own banner image on the menu, its own status line, prefix and language. It never looks like somebody else's bot. .setname, .setpp, .setlogo, .setbio.

The command list

Questions people ask

Does the bot read my private chats?

The bot is a linked device, like WhatsApp Web. It acts on the chats it is added to. Your messages are not written into a database — only counters (how many, when) so the dashboard can show activity. When anti-delete is switched on in a group, the last 200 group messages are held in memory so a deleted one can be reposted; that clears when the server restarts. Anything you deliberately store (notes, reminders, auto replies) is of course kept.

Do I lose my WhatsApp account?

No. Your number keeps working normally on your phone. The bot is one extra linked device, and you can unlink it any time from WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices.

Does my phone need to stay on?

No. The bot runs on a cloud server. After the QR scan it keeps working with your phone switched off.

Can I use it in a group where I am not admin?

Media, tools, AI and utilities work everywhere. Group protection and admin commands only work where the bot is made an admin — that is a WhatsApp rule, not a bot rule.

What if WhatsApp blocks the number?

Sending bulk, spam or unwanted messages can get a number limited. The bot has a per-user download cap and rate limits for that reason. Follow the setup rules we send and keep the bot out of spam groups.

Can I get a command that is not on the list?

Yes. Custom auto-reply commands and your own short commands are built in (.addcmd, .autoreply), so most requests need no coding at all. Bigger new features are discussed on WhatsApp.

How do I renew?

Message the same WhatsApp number before your period ends and pay again. Lifetime never renews.
